The mark

One stroke and a point

One stroke draws a page and a G, and stops at a point. The page is what an agent makes, the G is where it is kept, and the point is the gist.

A page
The stroke starts at the folded corner and follows the outline of a sheet.
A G
It turns inward before closing, so the sheet is also the initial of Gist.
Open
The outline never closes. The format is open: anyone can write an artifact, or display one.
The point
Where the stroke ends. The gist of something is its point, and here it is highlighted.

How it is drawn

  1. 1 A sheet with a folded corner, as every file icon draws it.

  2. 2 The outline opens below the fold and turns inward. It is now a G.

  3. 3 The stroke ends at a point, on the vertical axis of the sheet.

Sheet
54 × 70 units, measured on the centre line of the stroke
Stroke
8.5 units, with round ends and joins
Corners
radius 10; the fold cuts 18 units off two edges, at 45°
Point
two strokes wide, centred on the end of the stroke
Versions

Mark and name

The wordmark is Geist Semibold. Its capitals are centred on the mark and stand 0.36 of its height; the gap between them is one fifth of the mark's height.

Space and size

Room to breathe, two drawings

Below 32 px the stroke of the mark gets thin, so small sizes use a second, heavier drawing whose edges land on whole pixels at 16 px.

Keep clear space of x, one quarter of the mark's height, on every side of the mark and of the logo. Nothing else enters it: no text, no edge, no other logo.

Colour

Ink, Paper and one marker

The palette is black, white and three greys. Marker is the only colour, and it does the one thing a highlighter does.

On light grounds

  • Ink#0B0B0C--ga-light-ink

    The stroke, the wordmark and text.

  • Marker#F2A900--ga-light-marker

    The point in the mark, and a highlight behind Ink text. Never the colour of text, never a large area.

  • Ground#FFFFFF--ga-light-ground

    Preferred light ground. Paper.

  • Wash#F4F4F5--ga-light-wash

    Quiet surfaces: sidebars, code blocks, hovered rows.

  • Rule#E4E4E7--ga-light-rule

    Hairlines and borders.

  • Graphite#63636B--ga-light-graphite

    Secondary text.

On dark grounds

  • Ink#F4F4F5--ga-dark-ink

    The stroke, the wordmark and text.

  • Marker#FFB81C--ga-dark-marker

    The point in the mark, and a highlight behind dark text. Lifted for dark grounds.

  • Ground#0B0B0C--ga-dark-ground

    Preferred dark ground. The light scheme's Ink.

  • Wash#18181B--ga-dark-wash

    Quiet surfaces: cards, sidebars, code blocks.

  • Rule#27272A--ga-dark-rule

    Hairlines, borders and hovered rows.

  • Graphite#A1A1AA--ga-dark-graphite

    Secondary text.

Using Marker

  • Marker has two uses: the point in the mark, and a highlight behind dark text.
  • It is never the colour of text, and never fills a large area.
  • Highlight a phrase, not a paragraph. One per screen is usually enough.
  • Everything else is Ink, Graphite and the three neutrals.

Type

One family

Geist for everything that is read, Geist Mono for everything that is typed.

  • Sentence case everywhere: headings, buttons and labels.
  • Two weights on a screen are usually enough: Regular with Semibold, or Regular with Medium.
  • Set numbers that line up in columns with tabular figures.
  • Small labels in Geist Mono may be upper case, spaced out by 8%.

Geist and Geist Mono are in fonts/ under the SIL Open Font License, and on Google Fonts. The wordmark in the logo files is outlined, so they need no font installed.

Usage

Do and don't

Do

  • Use the light versions on light grounds and the dark versions on dark grounds.
  • Use the one-colour mark for single-colour printing, on photographs, and where a page sets its own colours.
  • Keep clear space of a quarter of the mark's height on every side.
  • At 24 px and below, use the compact mark.

The name

  • Write Gist Artifacts, two words with capitals, for the project and the format.
  • One of them is an artifact, in lower case. Gist keeps its capital: it is GitHub's name for the thing an artifact is stored in.
  • gist-artifacts is for code: the plugin, the tool and its commands.

Don't

Never add shadows, glows, gradients or outlines; never rotate the mark; never rebuild the wordmark in another typeface; and never set the mark beside GitHub's logos in a way that suggests the project is GitHub's.
